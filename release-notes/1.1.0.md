- Tiles now show the entity's icon by default, coloured when on, like Home Assistant's own tiles. To keep the bar down the left edge instead, set "State shown by" to the bar (`indicator: bar`).
- With the bar, you can show grey icons too, with the new "Show the icon" setting (`show_icon: true`).
- The colour when on now follows your theme's colour for each kind of entity, such as lights or switches.
